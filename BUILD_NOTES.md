# WHITE NOISE — build notes

- Genre / tone: winter psychological thriller, cozy horror, analog melancholy.
- Camera: first-person, smoothed movement, restrained head bob.
- Primary spaces: ranger cabin interior, snowy treeline, deer encounter, cliff edge, dark sea.
- Narrative interaction: shortwave radio, firewood, porch light, field lantern, basement cassette.
- Audio: original generated ambient music bed plus Web Audio effects for footsteps, receiver static, door movement and knocks.
- No external art packages or copyrighted music are included. Three.js is loaded from its official npm CDN at runtime under its MIT license.
- Dangerous equipment is only inert environmental set dressing. The night visitor is handled as an abstract, non-graphic supernatural encounter rather than a realistic combat or killing mechanic.
